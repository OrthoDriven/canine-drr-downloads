First launch

These builds aren't signed yet, so your computer asks once before opening Canine DRR.

  • macOS: open Canine DRR, click Done when told it can't be verified, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Canine DRR.
  • Windows: when SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Canine DRR tells you when a newer version is out; download it here and install it over this one.
